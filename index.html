About

I am an MPhil student at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), where I am supervised by Yingcong Chen and co-supervised by Dengbo He. I am also an admitted PhD student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I will be supervised by Bo Zhao. My research centers on Embodied AI, with broader interests in Spatial Intelligence, Computer Vision, and Multimodal Learning. I have worked on topics ranging from medical image analysis to vision-language-action modeling, and I am particularly interested in how perception, language, and action can be aligned for embodied systems that reason about 3D environments and support robust interaction in real-world settings.
